Welcome to my site!

I am a data analyst particularly interested in the ways quantitative methodologies can illuminate human behavior. I graduated with a BA in Political Science. Following college, I worked at the University of Pennsylvania as a research coordinator on various quantitative political science projects, and co-authored numerous research articles on public opinion and electoral politics. Beyond academic work, my journalistic writing has been published, among other venues, at the Washington Monthly, The Jewish Exponent, and The Forward.

I also have a lovely cat named Gal (I promise I did not name her myself). If you are very curious you can click on the button at the top right.
